# Monster Cookies

Yield: ~72 6" cookies

Great for bake sales. You'll need your two largest mixing bowls or pots. You might want to also get some plastic sandwitch bags to individually wrap them. It's useful to have double the number of cookie sheets as you'll put in oven so you can prep the next batch while the previous cools on the trays.


| Ingredient | Full Batch | Half Batch |
|------------|------------|-----------|
| Butter, softened | 1 lb | 1/2 lb |
| White sugar | 4 cups | 2 cups |
| Brown sugar | 2 lbs | 1 lb |
| Peanut butter | 3 lbs | 1 1/2 lbs |
| Eggs | 12 | 6 |
| Baking soda | 8 tsp | 4 tsp |
| Vanilla | 1 Tbsp | 1/2 Tbsp |
| Light corn syrup | 1 Tbsp | 1/2 Tbsp |
| Rolled oats | 18 cups | 9 cups |
| M&Ms | 2 lbs | 1 lb |
| Chocolate chips | 2 lbs | 1 lb |
| Peanuts | 1 lb | 1/2 lb |

1) Heat oven to 350 F.
2) Cream the butter (so) with the sugars with a stand or hand mixer. Mix in the peanut butter. 
3) Add the eggs, baking soda, vanilla, and corn syrup.
4) Split the mixture between your two largest bowls. Add 9 cups of oats to one and 9 to the other. Mix well.
5) Add half the M&Ms, chocolate chips, and peanuts to each bowl. It will be very thick. Mix well. Chill in the fridge for an hour or up to overnight.
6) Line your baking sheets with parchment paper.
7) Scoop out cookie dough with a large serving spood and pack tightly in your hands like a snowball to bind it together. Each ball should be about 100g. Place on the baking sheet and smoosh the ball flat with the palm of your hand. 
8) Bake for ~20 mins in the oven, rotating the trays halfway between. The cookies should just be starting to brown.
9) Remove trays and let the cookies cool on the trays, since the cookies can fall apart when too hot.
